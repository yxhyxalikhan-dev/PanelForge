The Obsidian Citadel had stood silent above the misty canyon for seven hundred years, its jagged spires piercing the low-hanging thunderclouds. At the edge of the fractured stone precipice stood Ren, his black travelling cloak whipping in the gale. The wind carried the metallic scent of approaching lightning.

Ren tightened the leather gauntlets around his hands. Beside him, Aoi rested her fingers on the pommel of her ancestral broadsword. Her silver hair glimmered against the gathering gloom.

Aoi: "The barrier frequency is failing, Ren. Look at the perimeter runes!"

Ren stepped forward, peering down into the abyss where deep violet runes flickered erratically along the fortress foundation. The ancient ward was decaying, letting loose an unsettling subsonic vibration that shook the earth beneath their boots.

Ren: "They broke the seal from the inside. We are already too late for negotiations."

Suddenly, the great iron gates of the citadel shattered outward with a deafening roar. Shockwaves tore across the courtyard, blasting centuries of dust into the tempestuous air. From the churning darkness emerged a towering titan forged of dark iron and glowing crimson sigils.

Aoi: "Take evasive maneuvers! It's an ancient Dread Warden!"

Ren drew his twin runic blades in a single fluid arc. Electric blue sparks crackled down the edges of the steel, illuminating his determined expression.

Ren: "Hold the flank, Aoi! I will strike the core rune on its chest!"

With a thunderous leap, Ren propelled himself into the air, blades trailing arcs of brilliant cerulean fire as the dread warden swung its massive spiked mace downward to crush him.

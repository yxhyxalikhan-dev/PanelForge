The sea of the Grand Frontier churned with violent emerald waves under a sky dark with swirling typhoon clouds. Perched upon the carved wooden figurehead of the pirate ship Leviathan stood Captain Kael, his battered red coat snapping wildly against the ocean wind. 

Below on the wet deck, his first mate Mara gripped the ship's wheel with both hands, steering into the monstrous swells.

Mara: "The whirlpool ahead is tearing the hull apart, Kael! We can't punch through this gale!"

Kael adjusted his straw-stitched captain's hat and grinned with fearless determination, looking straight into the heart of the vortex where legendary sea monsters coiled in the deep.

Kael: "A true adventurer never turns the rudder back! Set full sails forward!"

Suddenly, a massive sea beast erupted from the whirlpool, its razor-scaled serpent body towering three hundred feet above their mast, roaring into the storm.

Mara: "Incoming beast! All hands brace for impact!"

Kael leaped from the bowsprit straight into the sky, pulling back his fist as black lightning sparkled along his arm.

Kael: "Gale Fist... METEOR CRASH!"

With an earth-shattering shockwave, Kael struck the leviathan's armored skull, splitting the storm clouds above into golden daylight and carving an open passage across the mythical ocean.

🚀 Excited to share my latest autonomous AI project: PanelForge — an open-source Story-to-Manga & Comic Generation Engine!

Ever wondered how to turn a raw text adventure into a full graphic novel without weeks of manual storyboarding, grid calculations, and lettering?

I built PanelForge to automate the entire creative pipeline:
🌊 Narrative & Dialogue Breakdown: Parses character dialogue, detects camera angles (wide, close-up, dramatic action), and formats scenes.
📐 Mathematical Layout Grid: Computes dynamic multi-panel arrangements (classic 3-panel, balanced 4-panel grids, and dynamic action splashes).
🎨 Procedural Screentone & Ink Engine: An authentic manga shading, cross-hatching, and character staging engine that works completely offline with ZERO paid API costs.
💬 Auto-Fit Speech Balloon Lettering: Intelligently calculates font-fitting, rounded speech bubbles, shout balloons, and directional pointer tails.
📄 High-Resolution Multi-Page PDF: Automatically compiles publication-ready pages into a clean, downloadable PDF.
